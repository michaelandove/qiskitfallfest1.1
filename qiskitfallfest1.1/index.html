What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global collection of quantum computing events powered by IBM Quantum that empowers university students, researchers, and tech enthusiasts to delve into quantum hardware and software.

From beginner-friendly foundational sessions to hands-on programming with the Qiskit SDK and high-intensity hackathon challenges, this festival brings quantum tech within reach for everyone.
